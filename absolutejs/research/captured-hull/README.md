## Thin-cloud rank correction

The fixed1e-7 covariance rank heuristic also runs in the double implementation. Five captured thin clouds (including explicit4-point tetrahedra) can therefore take the two-sided planar triangulation path and yield negative signed boundary volume. The correction affects **only f64**: when covariance suggests rank below3, exact-sign orientation predicates look for a nonzero tetrahedral volume in the original points. A basis needs a linear scan, not all point quadruples. A certified full-dimensional cloud remains on the3D hull path; truly planar clouds keep their existing handling. Coordinates and visibility tolerances are unchanged. The f32 contact-shape constructor is unchanged.

Uses pinned robust1.2.0 predicates. [Shewchuk's adaptive predicates](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~quake/robust.html) explain how adaptive arithmetic obtains reliable orientation signs near degeneracy; this does not certify every later hull operation or remove arithmetic range limits. Our captured inputs are exact promoted Float32 coordinates. No random perturbation or volume-sign flipping is used.
